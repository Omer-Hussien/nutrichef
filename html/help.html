NutriChef Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

How are the nutrition macros calculated?
All calories, protein, carbs, fats, and fiber are auto-calculated based on the exact gram weight of the ingredients you select when adding a recipe.
Can I edit a recipe after publishing?
Yes! You can go to your recipe's detail page and click the Edit button. Only the original creator (or an Administrator) has permission to edit or delete a recipe.
How does the BMI calculator work?
Your Body Mass Index is calculated using the height and weight you provided in your profile settings. It automatically places you into the correct health category (Underweight, Normal, Overweight, or Obese).
